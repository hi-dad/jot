QUICKNOTE — capture thoughts into Apple Notes from anywhere
===========================================================

INSTALL
1. Drag "QuickNote" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click QuickNote.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
Your Mac will say it "can't verify" the app, because it isn't from the App Store.
That's normal for apps shared between friends. To allow it:
  1. Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to QuickNote.
  4. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again.
You only have to do this once.

PERMISSIONS
macOS asks for these the first time you use each feature:
- Input Monitoring: lets you hold Option or Control to open QuickNote. Turn
  QuickNote on in the page that opens, then quit and reopen QuickNote.
- Notes: on your first save. Click OK.
- Microphone and Speech Recognition: the first time you talk.

USING IT
- Look for the pencil icon at the top right of your screen (in the menu bar).
- Hold Option to type a note. Hold Control and talk; let go when you're done.
- Press Return to save it as a bullet in your note, or Esc to cancel.
- Control + Option + Space (type) and Control + Option + D (talk) also work.
- Click the pencil icon to choose which note your captures go to.

UNINSTALL
Click the pencil icon > Quit QuickNote, then drag QuickNote from Applications to the Trash.
