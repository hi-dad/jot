QUICKNOTE — capture thoughts into Apple Notes from anywhere
===========================================================

INSTALL
1. Drag "Jot" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click Jot.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
Your Mac asks if you're sure you want to open an app from the internet.
Click "Open". The app is signed and checked by Apple.

PERMISSIONS
macOS asks for these the first time you use each feature:
- Input Monitoring: lets you hold Option or Control to open Jot. Turn
  Jot on in the page that opens, then quit and reopen Jot.
- Notes: on your first save. Click OK.
- Microphone and Speech Recognition: the first time you talk.

USING IT
- Look for the pencil icon at the top right of your screen (in the menu bar).
- Hold Option to type a note. Hold Control and talk; let go when you're done.
- Press Return to save it as a bullet in your note, or Esc to cancel.
- Control + Option + Space (type) and Control + Option + D (talk) also work.
- Click the pencil icon to choose which note your captures go to.

UNINSTALL
Click the pencil icon > Quit Jot, then drag Jot from Applications to the Trash.
